# Cross-domain gradient prediction and computational efficiency

[Full original report (Chinese)](REPORT.md) · [English summary](../../predictor/countdown_predictor_results/scaling-domains-20260930/README.md) · [Reports index](../readme.md)

The study covers 13 model/task conditions, 501 completed training configurations and 156 frozen final-test versions. It examines downstream accuracy, warmup/update sensitivity, gradient quality and measured computation costs. Counts of configurations are not counts of independent statistical experiments.

The full report and its linked compact evidence are included here, together with all PNG/PDF figures. The meeting transcript and the full training workspace remain local.
