## Questions

1. Can one frozen neural gradient predictor transfer to new warmup initializations and data subsets?
2. Does a surrogate rollout correctly rank candidate training subsets, and how much actual Oracle performance is lost by choosing its preferred subset?
3. Does it save wall time at comparable quality, including predictor preparation and selection evaluation costs?

Matched Oracle16 isolates the predictor's extra contribution. Independently tuned Oracle32 and shorter true-gradient rollouts are essential efficiency/selection controls. Warmup is the shared no-additional-training baseline. Raw-gradient unbiasedness does not imply unbiased Adam updates or downstream accuracy.

Comparisons are paired within each start; variability across the three pairs combines warmup and update-order variation. Heldout-start downstream outcomes were not used to select methods.
- Neural predictor fitting uses source-study predictor_train and predictor_dev groups only. The original p123 predictor is fixed across new starts; new models use declared development teacher states.
- Five uniform, equal-size 1024-example subsets of the original 4097-example update pool are fixed before outcomes; overlaps between candidate training subsets are permitted.
- Candidate0 also supports development recipe selection. The remaining four random-subset combinations are new combinations of the original pool, not unseen training-example populations. The five genre candidates provide the separate group-disjoint training-data transfer condition.
- A secondary candidate family contains 1024 unseen training examples from each of five natural MNLI genres, with identical label counts. These groups are disjoint from original fitting/update/evaluation groups and from all new selection/evaluation groups. This is a more substantial composition shift than the random subsets.
- Fresh selection_dev: 1026 examples/338 source groups; fresh selection_holdout: 2049 examples/662 source groups. These are unused MNLI **training-population** groups, not the standard validation_matched benchmark. The latter remains a separately identified exploratory benchmark if used.
- Source accuracy_dev supports method screening. Selection_dev chooses candidate subsets after recipe freezing. Selection_holdout was read only after candidate choices were frozen, for final capability and selection-regret validation. Candidate-selection labels never fit an adaptation/predictor.
